Form guardrails

Quality first

  1. 01Leave 1–3 clean reps in reserve. Stop before technique collapses.
  2. 02Use a pain rule. Muscle effort is expected; sharp joint pain is a stop sign.
  3. 03Progress one variable. Add reps, a set, or a harder variation—not all three at once.
  4. 04Recover. Sleep 8–10 hours when possible and keep hard strength days non-consecutive.
